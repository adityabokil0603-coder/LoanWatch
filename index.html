How to use LoanWatch

  1. Enter the principal, annual interest rate, and tenure in the calculator.
  2. Review the estimated EMI, total repayment, interest, and schedule.
  3. Add a name, category, and start date, then save these calculator details as a loan.
  4. In your loan card, mark an instalment paid only after you have paid your lender. Review the payment history and remaining instalments.
  5. Download a report for an individual loan when you need a local summary.

All calculations are estimates using a reducing-balance model. Your lender’s schedule, fees, and actual payment dates may differ.

Financial glossary

Principal
The amount borrowed, before interest.
EMI
A regular monthly payment toward interest and principal.
Interest rate
The yearly percentage used to estimate the cost of borrowing.
Tenure
The planned length of the loan, usually measured in months.
Total repayment
All estimated instalments added together.
Total interest
The estimated borrowing cost, in addition to principal.
Outstanding balance
Estimated principal not yet repaid according to the tracked schedule.
Reducing-balance interest
Interest is estimated on the principal still outstanding each month.
Amortisation schedule
A month-by-month estimate of each payment’s interest and principal parts.
